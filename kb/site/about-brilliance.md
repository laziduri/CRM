# About Brilliance Advisory

Brilliance Advisory is a Singapore-based financial consultancy that provides human-led advisory services for personal and business loans.

## Our Approach

We don't just recommend all banks and apply to every loan. We do proper assessments, provide real advice, and create strategic plans tailored to your unique situation.

## What We Offer

- Proper Assessment & Analysis: We conduct thorough assessments of your financial position, requirements, and objectives
- Real Strategic Advice: Our consultants provide genuine, strategic advice tailored to your circumstances
- Selective Bank Approach: We identify and approach only the most suitable lenders based on your specific profile
- End-to-End Case Management: From initial assessment through structuring, bank liaison, negotiation, and final disbursement

## Our Network

We work with over 40 financial partners and grant agencies to help you secure the highest possible approval.
