# Brilliance Advisory Services

Brilliance Advisory helps businesses secure the right funding and grants to grow confidently. We handle everything from identifying what you qualify for, preparing your application, and managing the entire process until approval.

## Services We Provide

### Grant Advisory
We manage the full process from assessment to approval for various Singapore government grants.

### Loan Advisory
We connect you with the right financial partners to help you secure higher approval amounts with terms that fit your business goals.

### Consulting Approach
Our goal is to make funding simple, stress-free, and successful for every client. We focus on matching you with suitable programs, preparing strong submissions, and ensuring your applications are presented accurately and professionally.
